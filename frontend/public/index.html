                      Terms and Conditions

                      Terms & Conditions

                      Last Updated: October 28, 2026

                      Welcome to ARISETO, a Progressive Web Application (PWA) developed for small-scale farmers and farm owners of the Mindoro Progressive Multi-Purpose Cooperative (MPMPC) in Occidental Mindoro. By registering for or using ARISETO, you agree to these Terms & Conditions.

                      1. Account Creation & Approval

                      • Registration Requirements: You must provide your first and last name, required mobile number, MPMPC member number, and municipality/barangay. Providing an email address is optional.
                      • Admin Approval: New accounts are set to a pending status upon registration. You can only access the app after an MPMPC Cooperative Administrator approves your account.
                      • Account Security: Passwords must be 8 to 128 characters. You are responsible for keeping your credentials safe; login details must not be shared.

                      2. User Roles & Farm Management

                      • Account Types: There are two account types: Member (Farmer/Farm Owner) and Cooperative Administrator.
                      • Farm Ownership: Members can register farms and assign fields. The person who registers a farm becomes its Owner and can grant access to other farmers/workers.

                      3. Online & Offline Capabilities

                      • Offline Features: The Crop Calendar (task schedule) and Expense Tracker are planned to work offline. Planned auto-sync features will transmit tasks or expenses logged offline once your device reconnects to the internet.
                      • Online Features: AI Leaf Disease Detection, Yield Estimation, Advisory Chatbot, and Weather Alerts require an active internet connection.

                      4. AI Features & Advisory Disclaimer

                      • Crop Disease Detection & Advisory Chatbot: Recommendations provided by the AI disease scanner and chatbot are based on standard agricultural guides (such as PhilRice and Department of Agriculture sources).
                      • No Guarantee of Results: AI analyses are automated guidance tools. Users should use their best judgment and consult cooperative agricultural technicians for severe crop issues.

                      5. Weather Information & Notifications

                      • Weather forecasts and push alerts rely on your selected barangay center coordinates rather than exact GPS tracking.
                      • Planned push notifications pass through your browser's push service to alert you regarding weather-sensitive farming tasks (e.g., fertilization).

                      6. Account Deactivation & Requests

                      • MPMPC Administrators reserve the right to deactivate accounts that breach cooperative policies.
                      • You may submit a request for account deletion at any time via your profile. Upon administrator processing, your personal details will be removed, while anonymized farm performance records are kept for cooperative reporting.

                      Privacy Policy

                      Privacy Policy

                      Last Updated: October 28, 2026

                      ARISETO respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data in accordance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) of the Philippines.

                      1. Information We Collect

                      We collect the following information when you use ARISETO:

                      • Personal & Account Data: First and last name, required mobile number, optional email address, MPMPC member number, municipality, and barangay.
                      • Farm & Crop Data: Farm names, field sizes, planted crops/varieties, sowing dates, recorded activities, and farm expenses/harvest income.
                      • Photos: Leaf images uploaded for crop disease diagnosis.
                      • Push Notification Subscriptions: Web push tokens used to route weather and task alerts through your browser's push service.

                      2. How We Use Your Information

                      Your data is used to:

                      • Verify your identity and MPMPC membership.
                      • Provide crop activity calendars, yield estimates, and financial expense tracking.
                      • Process leaf photos for disease identification and display treatment steps.
                      • Deliver weather forecasts and weather-sensitive task alerts tailored to your barangay.
                      • Generate aggregated crop health and yield summary reports for MPMPC administrators.

                      3. Location Data Handling

                      ARISETO does not track your real-time GPS location. The system uses only the center coordinates of your selected barangay to retrieve localized weather forecasts.

                      4. Third-Party Data Sharing & Data Security

                      • Third-Party Services: We do not sell your personal data to advertisers. External services receive data strictly to power application features:
                        • OpenWeatherMap: Receives only your barangay's center coordinates to fetch weather reports.
                        • Browser Push Service: Handles token transmission to deliver web push alerts to your device.
                      • Cooperative Administration & Reports: MPMPC Cooperative Administrators can see your name, mobile number, email address (if provided), member number, municipality, and barangay to approve and manage accounts. They also access farm yields and disease reports to track crop health across the cooperative.
                      • Data Security: Your password is stored only as a one-way Argon2id hash and is never saved in plain text. Uploaded photos are stored securely in dedicated file storage.

                      5. Data Retention

                      Personal information is retained as long as your account remains active. When an account deletion request is processed by an MPMPC Administrator, personal identifying details are removed from the system, while anonymized farm performance and yield data are retained for historical cooperative reporting.

                      6. Your Rights as a Data Subject

                      Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012, you have the right to:

                      • Access & Correct: View and correct your personal details in the app; to correct farm records, contact the MPMPC administrator until field editing is available.
                      • Request Erasure: Submit a formal request for account deletion.
                      • Lodge Complaints: File a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (NPC) if you feel your data privacy rights have been violated.

                      7. Contact & Privacy Inquiries

                      For privacy concerns, data correction requests, or account deletion inquiries, please contact the MPMPC administrator handling privacy requests or visit the MPMPC Main Office, Occidental Mindoro.
